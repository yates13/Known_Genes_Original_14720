Genes were annotated in three stages. First, each of the 14,720 known genes was searched by BLASTX (BLAST+ 2.17.0; E-value < 1e-5) against SwissProt using the gene’s own genomic sequence, and the best hit per gene was assigned (10,799 genes), with a transposon-aware choice between the new search and a previous transcript-based search. Second, genes lacking a SwissProt label were searched by DIAMOND against protein sets from Culex quinquefasciatus, Aedes aegypti, Anopheles gambiae and Drosophila melanogaster; the highest-bitscore non-vague name was assigned (2,227 genes; 1,694 remain unannotated). Third, each label was given a confidence rating: SwissProt labels are high at E < 1e-20 and medium otherwise; mosquito-derived labels are rated by the ratio of the label’s bitscore to the gene’s best bitscore (≥ 0.8 high, 0.5-0.8 medium, < 0.5 low). Low-confidence mosquito names, and names containing DUF or LOC identifiers, were replaced with “uncharacterized protein” with the original name retained as an attribute. The ratings were checked by aligning label proteins to 37 genes with miniprot. Per-database E-values and P-values (P = 1 − e^(−E)) are provided in the gene BED and GTF files. Scripts are at github.com/yates13/Known_Genes_Original_14720.
